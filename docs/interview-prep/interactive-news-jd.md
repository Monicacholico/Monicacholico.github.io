## About the Role

Interactive News Technology is a team of developer-journalists embedded in the New York Times newsroom. They power new story forms and build tools for newsgathering, while supporting breaking news, exclusive reporting, and major events.

They're looking for a curious and creative associate software engineer to pioneer new features in newsroom projects used by hundreds of NYT journalists and millions of readers. Possible work: systems that power real-time data for elections and polling coverage, newsroom AI product development, software behind document research, reader submission collection, or projects powered by reader data like quizzes, news games, and WordleBot.

**Stack:** JavaScript, using Node.js, TypeScript, React and sometimes Svelte. PostgreSQL for storage/analysis. Hosted Google Cloud services and serverless technologies, and AI tools such as Cursor and Claude.

> "The Interactive News Technology team works as part of the newsroom, but this is **not a data visualization or reporting role**."

## Responsibilities
- Prototype new ideas to help journalists report and tell stories
- Contribute to software features from technical design through live operation
- Participate in project research and technical design as part of your project team
- Build features that are reliable, scalable and secure
- Work with newsroom desks to explore bold reporting avenues relating to data, documents or novel story forms
- Collaborate with teams across the newsroom including A.I. Initiatives, Data Journalism, and Graphics
- Ensure that systems provide clear and accurate information to reporters and readers
- Sometimes work on tight deadlines around the news
- Take part in skill-building and learning opportunities
- Support the value of journalistic independence and the mission

## Basic Qualifications
- At least 1 year of experience building web applications in JavaScript (TypeScript preferred)
- Experience contributing to newsgathering, reporting, editing, or news presentation processes
- Experience taking a technical project from concept to completion (concept definition, technical design, technical implementation)
- Experience working as part of a team to develop software
- Experience with A.I. software development best practices
