I've taken projects from concept through technical design to production, including reusable template systems that other teams depend on. This work has given me a strong foundation in building responsive layouts and reliable, scalable web applications within the standards and pace of this organization.

I'm eager to apply these engineering skills to journalism. I'm a curious, creative person with a backlog of data-driven story ideas I want to build. I'm driven to understand, learn and share how the world works — something I think comes from my humanities background, including a bachelor's in law — and I think editorially, question constantly and look for ways technology can help people better understand the world.

I truly believe people make better decisions when they have information on their side. Outside of work, I've been building toward this on my own time. Planeta Mujer (www.planeta-mujer.org) is an interactive platform I designed and coded from scratch. To share these subjects through scrollytelling, interaction and visualization, I've been exploring and teaching myself technologies outside my daily work such as D3.js, the Canvas API, Three.js and GLSL. I enjoy the whole process, from prototyping an idea to implementing it into something real. I also write bilingual explanatory articles for a blog I've run since 2014.

I hold a master's in gender and social policy. In 2025 I completed a course in Statistics and Probability for Data Analysis; this year I finished a graduate diploma in digital experiences, marketing and ecosystems; and I'm currently working toward NYU's Modern Journalism certificate, which I'll complete at the end of the year.
